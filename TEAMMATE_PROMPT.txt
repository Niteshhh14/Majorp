PROJECT PURPOSE
Prepare clean/noisy speech at 16 kHz, suppress noise with spectral subtraction, then apply a variable-bandwidth (CVBW) audiogram fitting stage. Evaluate with SNR, SI-SDR, optional STOI/PESQ/HASPI, and response plots. The point is useful speech for hearing-loss profiles, not just a matched frequency response.

WHAT EXISTS
- main.m prepares speech/noise audio and runs the default audiogram preset 4 pipeline.
- run_full_pipeline.m coordinates denoising, CVBW processing, metrics, WAV/MAT output, and reports.
- DSP/ includes spectral_subtraction_enhance.m, apply_cvbw_hearing_aid.m, get_cvbw_preset.m, prototype/Farrow code.
- run_evaluation_grid.m currently covers 12 cases; the report describes a possible 480-case grid.
- Available_source_code/ and Base_papers/ contain supplied reference material.
- Results/Samples/sample_01 ... sample_10 contain ten separate 2.5-second runs. Large datasets/generated binary outputs are excluded from Git; see .gitignore and WALKTHROUGH.md.
